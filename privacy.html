Privacy Policy

Last updated November 12, 2026

What we collect

  • Sign-up information: your name, business name, trade, revenue range, email, phone, service area and domain.
  • The content you give us for your website: logo, photos, text and documents.
  • Payment details, which are handled by Stripe. We do not see or store your full card number.
  • Site activity records: whether your site is up and, if you buy call tracking, the number of calls from your website.
  • Emails you send us.

How we use it

To decide whether to offer you the service, build and run your website, bill you, report to you each month, and respond to you.

Who else handles it

We use service providers to run Site Castle: Stripe (payments), Netlify (website hosting and this sign-up form), Google (file storage and email), and CallRail if you buy call tracking. They process your information for us. We do not sell your information.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while you are a client and for one year after you cancel, then delete it, except where we must keep records for tax or legal reasons.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your information by emailing kyle@sitecastle.net.
